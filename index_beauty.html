  1. 2023

    Tamper detection & localisation 图像篡改检测与定位

    Research assistant at DICALab, Sichuan University. IML‑ViT applied vision transformers with multi-scale features and edge supervision to manipulation localisation, while Perceptual MAE showed masked autoencoders can learn low-level forgery cues. First conference paper at ICONIP'23, and a face-recognition project at the NUS Summer School. 在四川大学 DICALab 担任研究助理。IML‑ViT 用视觉 Transformer 结合多尺度特征与边缘监督做篡改定位，Perceptual MAE 证明掩码自编码器同样能学到低层伪造痕迹。发表首篇会议论文 ICONIP'23，并入选 NUS 暑期学校完成人脸识别项目。

    IML-ViTPerceptual MAEICONIP'23
  2. 2024

    From models to benchmarks 从做模型到做评测

    IMDL‑BenCo became a modular benchmark and codebase covering both detection and localisation with 8 baselines and GPU-accelerated evaluation — published in the NeurIPS 2024 Benchmark Track with a Spotlight award. Alongside it, Beyond Visual Appearances used hybrid graph reasoning for privacy-sensitive object identification. Graduated as Outstanding Graduate, then started the PhD at Hong Kong PolyU. IMDL‑BenCo 做成覆盖检测与定位两大任务的模块化基准与代码库，含 8 个基线与 GPU 加速评估，在 NeurIPS 2024 基准赛道发表并获 Spotlight 奖。同期 Beyond Visual Appearances 用混合图推理做隐私敏感物体识别。获四川大学与四川省优秀毕业生，随后赴香港理工大学攻读博士。

    IMDL-BenCoNeurIPS 2024 SpotlightPrivacy-sensitive Objects

    Benchmarking hierarchical reasoning 为层次化推理立基准

  4. 2025

    Agents that actually retrieve 真正会检索的智能体

    The question became whether an agent can finish the job: QA‑Dragon implemented query-aware dynamic RAG for knowledge-intensive visual question answering, choosing the retrieval strategy per query. I led the team at the Meta CRAG‑MM multimodal retrieval challenge and placed 12th globally among hundreds of teams, and gave an invited talk at KDD Reasoning Day on hierarchical data and RAG. 问题变成了「智能体能不能真的把事做完」：QA‑Dragon 为知识密集型视觉问答实现查询感知的动态 RAG，按问题动态选择检索策略。带队参加 Meta CRAG‑MM 多模态检索挑战赛，在数百支队伍中取得全球第 12 名，并受邀在 KDD Reasoning Day 报告层次化数据与 RAG。

  6. 2026

    Agents in production 在生产环境里跑起来的智能体

    At Kuaishou E-commerce I put LLMs into a live ranking system. AIR (KDD'26 ADS Track) distils cross-domain user behaviour into atomic intents offline and composes them online, lifting cart-attached GMV by about 7pp and live-avatar GMV by about 5pp, with roughly 20pp on coarse ranking. A/B Agent closes the loop with a self-evolving agent for strategy optimisation. 在快手电商把大模型做进真实排序链路。AIR（KDD'26 ADS Track）离线用大模型把跨域用户行为蒸馏为原子意图、在线高效组合并服务，精排挂车 GMV 提升约 7pp、Live 头像约 5pp，粗排约 20pp；A/B Agent 则以自进化智能体完成策略闭环优化。

    AIR · KDD'26 ADSA/B Agent+7pp cart GMV

Research Topics 研究方向

My research centers on large language models (LLMs), AI agents, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), with a particular interest in turning LLM reasoning into reliable, efficient systems for large-scale decision-making and personalized user experiences.

My previous research was primarily focused on topics within computer vision, such as tampering detection and object recognition tasks. I have contributed to the design of high-impact benchmarks, such as HiBench, and comprehensive surveys like WebAgents. My work has been published in top-tier conferences, including NeurIPS 2024 (spotlight), KDD 2025, KDD 2026 and AAAI 2025, and has accumulated 500+ citations, with an h-index of 6.

我的研究兴趣主要集中在大语言模型（LLMs）、AI 智能体和检索增强生成（RAG），尤其关注如何将大模型推理能力转化为可靠、高效的系统，用于大规模决策与个性化用户体验。

我之前的研究主要集中在计算机视觉领域，如图像篡改检测和目标识别任务。我为高影响力基准测试的设计做出了贡献，如HiBench，以及综合性调研如WebAgents。我的工作发表在顶级会议上，包括NeurIPS 2024（spotlight）, KDD 2025, KDD 2026和AAAI 2025，累计获得500+引用，h指数为6。

Retrieval-Augmented Generation 检索增强生成

Structure-aware RAG for knowledge-intensive and multimodal queries. 面向知识密集与多模态查询的结构化 RAG。

Evaluation & Benchmarks 评测与基准测试
